## final verification notes (additive)

Added after the focused test runs R-13 to R-20 recorded in [ACCEPTANCE_TEST_REPORT.md](ACCEPTANCE_TEST_REPORT.md). The rows above are unchanged. Statuses are kept in [REQUIREMENTS_STATUS.md](REQUIREMENTS_STATUS.md).

- **Test evidence.** The integration and unit test classes cited in the "Integration test" column all passed on 2026-10-29 with a mocked model and isolated databases (50 acceptance-scenario tests, 233 critical-workflow tests, 143 unit tests, 14 task-edit tests). The recordings, screenshots and exported plans cited in the "Supporting evidence" column were not inspected.
- **FR10 (export).** The export code (`PlanExportService.cs`) writes the draft and job IDs, approval time, the approved task placements with technicians and status, and progress. It does **not** write assumptions, evidence references or deferred work, which the requirement lists. FR10 is therefore Partial, although the test `AT11_ExportJson_And_Pdf_MatchContent` passes (it checks the content that is exported).
- **FR04.** The two alternatives and their completion time, labour cost, deferred tasks and cost warning are shown. There is no explicit statement when fewer than two alternatives exist, and the trade-off text is the scheduling-policy sentence.
- **FR07.** Replanning, locks and revision linkage are implemented and their tests pass. No before-and-after comparison or explanation of material changes exists.
- **FR09.** Planning-request submission and clarification are not recorded as business actions (no actor or time).
